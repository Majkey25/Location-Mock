Location Mock privacy policy

Effective August 28, 2026.

Location Mock does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Android approximate-location permission is used only while the user-started foreground service supplies mock coordinates. Selected coordinates and the latest service error remain in fixed-size local app preferences. No location history is kept. Android backup and device transfer are disabled.

The app has no Internet permission, advertising, analytics, account system, or maintainer-operated server.

Google Play uses com.majkeylab.locationmock. The GitHub APK uses com.majkeylab.locationmock.github. Both variants have the same local-only data behavior.

The Open Proton VPN button starts independent software or its Google Play listing. Location Mock does not receive VPN traffic or data and has no affiliation with Proton AG.
