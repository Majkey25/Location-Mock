Using Location Mock

This Android utility supplies test coordinates through Android's mock-location feature. Use it only for testing you are authorized to perform and follow the terms of services you test. Android marks coordinates as mock; other applications may detect or reject them. It does not prove physical presence, guarantee access, change an IP address, or guarantee prices.

Address search depends on the device's geocoding provider and may be unavailable or inaccurate. Do not rely on it for safety-critical navigation or emergencies. Review the privacy policy before sending address queries.

Costs and refunds

Location Mock has no in-app purchases, subscription, advertising or paid unlock. There is no app charge to refund. Your mobile carrier and any VPN service you choose can charge under their own agreements; contact that provider about those charges. This does not restrict mandatory consumer rights.

Separate distributions

The Google Play package com.majkeylab.locationmock has no VPN service. The GitHub package com.majkeylab.locationmock.github includes the WireGuard client. You supply the VPN configuration and choose the server. Location Mock does not sell or operate a VPN service. Review your VPN operator's terms and privacy policy before connecting.
