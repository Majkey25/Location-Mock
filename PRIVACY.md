# Privacy policy

Effective date: August 28, 2026

Location Mock does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The app uses Android approximate-location permission only while its user-started foreground service supplies mock coordinates. The selected coordinates and latest service error stay in fixed-size local app preferences. The app keeps no location history. Android backup and device transfer are disabled.

Location Mock has no Internet permission, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, account system, or maintainer-operated server.

The **Open Proton VPN** button starts a separately installed Proton VPN app or opens its Google Play listing. Proton VPN is independent software governed by Proton AG's privacy policy and terms. Location Mock does not receive VPN traffic or data.
